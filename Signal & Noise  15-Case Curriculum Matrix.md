# Signal & Noise: The AI Enablement Coach

**A 5-Level, Mobile-First Gamified AI Learning Curriculum (Zero-AI Simulation)**

## 1\. Premise & Learner Persona

* **The Setting:** **Acme Flux**, a fast-growing, well-meaning company that just rolled out an internal AI workspace called **Cortex** to every department.

* **The Learner's Role:** **AI Enablement & Communications Coach**. Rather than troubleshooting IT bugs, you are coaching everyday colleagues (in HR, Marketing, Finance, Product, and Executive Comms) who are frustrated or baffled by how their new AI partner is behaving.

* **The Core Loop (Mobile 3-Tab Layout):**

  1. **The Situation (Observe):** Read the colleague's frustrated message and tap the flawed AI output to spot clues.

  2. **The Brief (Inspect):** Look at how the colleague actually communicated with (or set up) the AI.

  3. **The Coaching Workbench (Fix & Simulate):** Use tactile mobile mechanics—swapping briefing cards, pruning chat history, or snapping together playbooks—to coach the colleague toward a 5-star outcome.

## 3\. The 3x5 Interactive Interface (Gamification & Progression)

* **The Unified Home Screen (Grid & Field Guide):** Rather than jumping between different screens or swipeable decks, the entire app's main menu is a beautifully designed, static **3x5 stack of case cards** representing the 15 cases.

  1. **Locked Modules:** Cases further down the progression track are greyed out with a lock icon.

  2. **Active Modules:** The currently available case shows the title, a brief teaser, and a pulsing "Start Case" button.

  3. **The Flip Mechanic (The Field Guide):** When a user successfully clears a case, they earn that level's **Mastery Badge**, and that specific case card *flips over* permanently. The back of the card becomes the **Field Guide** resource detailing the core concept. The home screen grid ultimately transforms into a fully accessible, permanent reference library of AI concepts for the user.

* **Coaching Impact Score & Leaderboards:**

  * Learners earn **Clarity XP** for solving cases on the first simulation run and **Efficiency Bonuses** for finding all clues in the fewest taps.

  * Weekly cohort leaderboards track **"Colleagues Coached"** and **"First-Try Solves"** to spark friendly competition without penalizing experimentation.
