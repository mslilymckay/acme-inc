# Acme Flux AI Coach

**A 5-Level, Mobile-First Gamified AI Learning Curriculum (Zero-AI Simulation)**

## 1\. Premise, Style & Core Loop ("Golden Slice" Standard)

* **The Setting:** **Acme Flux**, a fast-growing, well-meaning company that just rolled out an internal AI workspace called **Cortex** to every department.

* **Visual & Metaphor Direction:** Mundane, relatable office satire (*Bob's Burgers* deadpan character portraits paired with universal office supplies—note cards, red pens, legal pads, highlighters, folders, and flowcharts—rather than literal environmental scenes or sci-fi/food metaphors).

* **The Learner's Role:** **AI Enablement & Communications Coach**. Rather than troubleshooting IT bugs, you are coaching everyday colleagues (in HR, Marketing, Operations, L\&D, Product, Comms, Strategy, Events, Customer Success, Sales, Research, and Design) who are frustrated or baffled by how their new AI partner is behaving.

* **The Core Loop (Mobile 3-Tab "Golden Slice" Layout):**

  1. **1\. Observe (The Incident):** Consistent across all 15 cases (matching 2.1.1). Displays the colleague's deadpan character portrait (in their stressed/frustrated state, free of emoji clutter) with their frustrated quote, paired with the flawed Cortex output containing tappable clue hotspots (+25 XP each).  
  2. **2\. Inspect (The Crime Scene):** Displays the exact prompt or setup the colleague used, paired with a diagnostic Warning Panel that previews the core mental model and leads into the workbench via the **"Open Workbench"** CTA.  
  3. **3\. Workbench (Fix & Simulate):** Uses simplified, tactile office-stationery interaction graphics (drawn from 7 core interaction archetypes) to fix the prompt or workflow. Players review their work and tap **"Simulate Cortex Prompt"** to trigger the completion modal—featuring the colleague's earnestly satisfied portrait (with tap-to-expand quote), the resolved prompt simulation, and the **"Unlock Coach Notes Card"** CTA.

## 2\. The 7 Simplified Workbench Interaction Archetypes

Rather than building bespoke environmental scenes for each case, all 15 workbenches use clean, reusable office-supply graphic components:

1. **4-Corner / Slot Docking (Cases 2.1, 4.1):** Bidirectional tap-to-match note cards or modular rules into a 4-quadrant planner or skill template.  
2. **Red-Pen / Pruning Tool (Cases 2.2, 3.2):** Tap conflicting prompt notes to strike/trash them, or tap the pivot point in a drifting chat log to fork a clean thread.  
3. **Card Swapper / File Injector (Cases 2.3, 3.1, 4.2):** Swap vague adjective notes, leading questions, or broad trigger labels for concrete file attachments, critical personas, or specific triggers.  
4. **Two-Color Highlighter Stamp (Case 3.3):** Stamp sections of a sensitive draft as **"AI Restructure"** vs. **"Human Rewrite"**.  
5. **Slider & Cutoff Dial (Cases 1.2, 1.3):** Slide a date marker to expose a model's training cutoff \+ toggle Web Search, or drag crop handles on an oversized document to fit the working memory window.  
6. **X-Ray Lens & File Attachment (Case 1.1):** Toggle an "X-Ray" switch on a draft to highlight probability-guessed buzzwords, then clip the real policy PDF to the prompt.  
7. **Flowchart & Gate Nodes (Cases 4.3, 5.1, 5.2, 5.3):** Connect a missing template node, sequence a 3-step vertical task chain, snap a stopping-condition rule onto a handoff arrow, or drop a "Human Approval Gate" lock before a broadcast step.

## 4\. The 3x5 Desk Interface & Progression

* **The Unified Desk View (`HomeView`):**  
  * **Coach Badges Header:** Displays all 5 Level Mastery Badges on a single row (`1/5 Earned`). A level badge unlocks only when **all three cases** in that level are closed.  
  * **Case History (3x5 Matrix):** Grouped into 5 levels with horizontal row dividers.  
  * **Two-State Case Cards:**  
    * **Open (Active / Unsolved):** Displays the case hook and icon; tapping opens the module (`[Coach] ->`).  
    * **Closed (Completed):** Flips to a green completed state; tapping opens the **Coach Notes** drawer (`[Notes] ->`) where players can review the concept or replay the case.  
  * **Sticky Action FAB:** Highlights the next open case (`Solve [Case ID]: [Case Name] ->`).
